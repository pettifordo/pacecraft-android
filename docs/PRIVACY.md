# CoachMeSimple privacy policy

Updated 14 November 2026. Contact: pettifordo@gmail.com.

CoachMeSimple calculates running plans on your Android phone. It has no CoachMeSimple account, advertising, analytics, AI service or coaching backend.

With your permission, the phone app reads exercise sessions and distance from Health Connect. Optional heart rate, elevation, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability improve effort/load analysis. Optional history permission allows up to 26 weeks of history; optional background access permits daily refresh. The app does not write or delete Health Connect records.

Goals, settings, feedback, manual runs and an imported history cache are stored in private app storage. The app excludes Android cloud backup and device transfer. Removing required Health Connect permissions removes its imported cache when permissions are next checked. Settings → Clear all local data deletes the phone app's stored history/settings; original Health Connect records remain with their source apps.

If you choose Send workout to Wear OS, the selected structured workout transfers through Google Play services' paired-device Data Layer. The Wear companion uses location for measured workout distance and optional body-sensor access for heart rate during a visibly started workout. It stores a paused recovery snapshot and transfers completed runs back to the phone. It does not upload a GPS route to a CoachMeSimple server. Uninstalling the watch app or clearing its app storage removes its local copy; finishing a workout stops sensor tracking.

If you share a PDF, calendar file or workout JSON, Android sends that file to the app/recipient you select. The receiving app's privacy policy then applies. Reminders are optional local notifications. No health data is sold or used for advertising.

Training estimates and flags are uncertain training guidance, not medical diagnosis. This policy describes the testing build; production Play disclosures must be reviewed against the final signed release and Google's current requirements.
