# Send this with the APK

1. Download the APK to your Android phone (Android 9+), tap it, and allow that browser/file app to install unknown apps if asked. Install **Pacecraft**. You can turn that installation permission off again afterwards.
2. Tap **Explore with sample history**. Open Next run, Progress, History and Coach. Check scrolling, readable text, and that every session's steps agree with its total. Settings → Exit sample history returns to your own data.
3. For real runs: open Health Connect on your phone, connect your recording app and allow it to write exercise and distance. In Pacecraft, connect Health Connect and grant exercise/distance, optionally heart rate/elevation and history. Import supports up to 26 weeks; older history permission may not exist on some Android versions.
4. If your watch app does not share into Health Connect, add runs manually. Race estimates require eight usable runs spanning at least six weeks and totalling at least 40 km, with usable outdoor efforts.
5. Set a goal and your available weekdays in Settings. Try a time cap and a weekly distance cap. Check that the next run respects them. Change units to miles and check that pace/distance convert together.
6. Submit a check-in with a niggle or soreness 4/5; the next run should avoid hard training. Skip a day; it should not immediately be offered again.
7. After a run reaches Health Connect, foreground Pacecraft or use History → Refresh. Inspect the run date, distance, time, source and optional heart rate. Same-day matching may link it to a previous prescription.
8. Close and reopen the app: settings and history should remain. Revoke exercise or distance access in Health Connect: returning to Pacecraft should clear imported run cache, retaining manual entries. Settings → Clear all local data deletes Pacecraft's store but leaves Health Connect originals untouched.

Please report phone model, Android version, recording app, steps to reproduce and expected/actual behaviour. Screenshots of the app and import message help; avoid sharing raw health records unless you choose to.

Known testing limits: the app does not record GPS, export workouts to a watch, or sync in the background. Estimates are provisional and training advice should not override pain or feeling unwell.
